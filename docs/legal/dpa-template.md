This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the Orbitly Terms of Service entered into between **Orbitly, Inc.** ("Processor") and the customer ("Controller") governing the processing of personal data in connection with the Orbitly Platform.

---

## 1. Scope & Roles of Parties
- **Customer as Controller:** The Customer determines the categories of social media data, content, and team member accounts submitted to Orbitly.
- **Orbitly as Processor:** Orbitly processes personal data solely on documented customer instructions to plan, draft, schedule, and publish content to third-party social media networks.

## 2. Security of Processing
Orbitly implements and maintains appropriate technical and organizational measures (TOMs) as specified in Annex II:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256-GCM for credentials, AES-256 for database volumes).
- Multi-tenant tenant boundary isolation preventing cross-workspace leakage.
- Automated daily encrypted backups retained for 30 days.
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and least-privilege principles.

## 3. Data Subject Rights & Assistance
Orbitly provides in-app tools enabling the Customer to fulfill Data Subject Requests (DSRs) under GDPR Chapter III, including exportable JSON data archives and automated account/workspace deletion routines.

## 4. Sub-Processors
Orbitly maintains an up-to-date list of authorized sub-processors at [https://orbitly.app/legal/sub-processors](https://orbitly.app/legal/sub-processors).
