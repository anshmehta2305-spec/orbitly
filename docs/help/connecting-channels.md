# Connecting Social Channels

Orbitly connects directly to official social platform APIs using secure OAuth authorization.

## Supported Social Networks
- **Instagram:** Professional or Creator Account connected to a Facebook Page.
- **X (Twitter):** Personal or Organization account.
- **LinkedIn:** Personal profiles and Company Pages.
- **Facebook:** Business Pages and Groups.
- **YouTube:** Channels for publishing Shorts and long-form videos.
- **TikTok:** Personal and Business creator accounts.
- **Threads:** Official Threads profile publishing.
- **Pinterest:** Business account boards.
- **Bluesky:** AT Protocol handle and app password.
- **Mastodon:** Any federated instance domain.
- **Telegram:** Channel broadcast bot.

## Channel Health Checks
Orbitly tests channel authorization every 6 hours in the background. If a platform invalidates a token (for example, after you change your password on that platform), an orange warning badge will appear next to the channel with a **"Reconnect"** button.
