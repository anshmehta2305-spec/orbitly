# Approval Workflows & Client Sharing

Orbitly includes enterprise-grade collaboration features for agencies and marketing teams.

## Internal Approvals
1. In **Settings > Approvals**, enable "Require approval before publishing".
2. When an **Editor** creates a post, the status becomes `PENDING_APPROVAL`.
3. Team **Admins** and **Owners** receive an alert in their **Approvals Inbox** (`/approvals`).
4. Approvers can:
   - **Approve:** Post immediately advances to `SCHEDULED` status.
   - **Request Changes:** Leaves threaded feedback notes for the author.
   - **Reject:** Terminal rejection with a feedback reason.

## External Client Share Links
1. Go to **Team > Client Share Links**.
2. Click **"Create Client Share Link"**.
3. Configure the link:
   - Restrict to specific channels or date ranges.
   - Optional password protection.
   - Optional expiration date.
4. External clients can view the calendar, leave comments, and approve posts without creating an Orbitly account.
