# AI Studio & Brand Voice

The Orbitly AI Studio powers your content generation workflows with multi-model AI.

## Features
- **Caption Generation:** Generate engaging posts from simple bullet points.
- **Content Repurposing:** Convert long YouTube transcripts, blog URLs, or newsletters into social post threads.
- **Smart Hashtag Clustering:** Recommend viral and niche hashtags based on post context.
- **Alt-Text Generator:** Generate accessible image descriptions automatically.
- **Brand Voices:** Train custom brand personas with tone traits (e.g., "professional yet approachable"), custom vocabulary, and prohibited words.

## AI Credits
- Each workspace receives a monthly allowance of AI credits based on its subscription tier (Free: 20, Pro: 200, Team: 1,000, Agency: 5,000).
- Credits reset on your billing renewal date.
- 1 standard text generation consumes 1 credit.
