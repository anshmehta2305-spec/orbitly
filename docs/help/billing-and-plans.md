# Billing, Subscriptions & Invoicing

Manage your workspace subscription, invoices, and payment details under **Settings > Billing** (`/settings/billing`).

## Plan Tiers
- **Free:** 3 channels, 30 scheduled posts/month, 1 seat, 20 AI credits.
- **Pro ($24/mo or $19/yr):** 10 channels, 300 posts/month, 3 seats, 200 AI credits, 14-day free trial.
- **Team ($69/mo or $55/yr):** 25 channels, 1,000 posts/month, 10 seats, approvals, client share links, 14-day free trial.
- **Agency ($199/mo or $159/yr):** 100 channels, 10,000 posts/month, 50 seats, white-label, Public API & MCP Server, 24/7 dedicated support.

## Upgrades, Downgrades & Proration
When upgrading, your unused credit on the current billing period is automatically calculated and deducted from the invoice.

## Payment Failures & 7-Day Grace Period
If your payment method fails, your account enters a **7-day grace period** (`PAST_DUE`). During these 7 days, your scheduled posts continue to publish normally while you update your card in the Stripe Customer Portal. If unpaid after 7 days, the workspace automatically downgrades to Free and excess scheduled posts are safely converted to drafts without deleting your data.
