# Getting Started with Orbitly

Welcome to Orbitly! This guide walks you through setting up your workspace in under 5 minutes.

## 1. Create Your Account & Workspace
1. Visit `https://orbitly.app/register` and sign up with email/password or Google/GitHub OAuth.
2. During onboarding, specify your workspace name and primary time zone. Orbitly supports multi-workspace management under one account.

## 2. Connect Your Social Media Channels
1. In the sidebar, click **Channels** (`/channels`).
2. Select your platform (Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon).
3. Complete the OAuth authorization window to grant publishing permissions.

## 3. Create Your First Cross-Platform Post
1. Click the **"Create Post"** button in the top navigation.
2. Select the channels you want to publish to.
3. Write your core copy in the global editor or tailor content per channel.
4. Pick a scheduled date and time or click **"Publish Now"**.
