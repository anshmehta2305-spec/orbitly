# Analytics & Reporting

Orbitly automatically tracks cross-network performance metrics to help optimize your social strategy.

## Tracked Metrics
- **Audience Reach & Impressions:** Total views across all connected channels.
- **Engagement Rate:** Likes, comments, retweets, and shares divided by total impressions.
- **Click-Throughs:** In-link clicks tracked across your campaign posts.
- **Best Time Heatmap:** Visual 7x24 grid showing the hours and days with peak audience interaction.

## Exporting Reports
Export comprehensive PDF or CSV summaries with one click for client reporting and executive presentations. Historical retention spans from 30 days (Free) up to 2 years (Agency).
